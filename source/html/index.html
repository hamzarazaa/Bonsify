The gap

of Shopify traffic is mobile. Most of it never converts the way desktop does.

A browser storefront asks a mobile customer to do more work for less reward.

Slower loads. No home screen presence. No push notifications. No offline access. Every extra tap is a place the sale quietly leaks out.

A native app closes that gap — higher repeat purchase rate, better retention, a direct line to the customer that doesn't depend on an algorithm or an inbox. Most stores never build one, because it's usually slow, expensive, and needs a dev team they don't have.

What would change if your store lived on the home screen instead of in a browser tab?
