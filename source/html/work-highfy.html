The brief

Acquisition was working. Retention was leaking.

Highfy was spending to bring shoppers in, then losing the relationship the moment they closed the tab.

With no home screen icon and no way to reach customers directly, every re-engagement meant paying for the same visitor twice. The fix wasn't more ads — it was a permanent, owned channel back to the people who'd already bought once.

What we built

A retention engine, not just a storefront

  • Native iOS and Android apps with the full Shopify catalog and checkout built in.
  • Push notifications as the core re-engagement channel — restocks, offers, and back-in-stock alerts.
  • Fast, app-native browsing that removes the load and friction of mobile web.
  • Launched on both stores and maintained, with iteration based on how customers actually use it.
