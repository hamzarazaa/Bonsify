The brief

A store customers loved, on a surface they rarely returned to

BachaParty had a healthy Shopify store and repeat customers — but almost all of them shopped on mobile, through a browser tab they had to find again every time.

There was no home screen presence, no push channel, and every return visit started from scratch. The brand needed the loyalty it had already earned to live somewhere permanent: an icon on the phone.

BachaParty
What we built

A native app that mirrors the store, not a wrapper around it

  • Native iOS and Android apps, synced directly to the existing Shopify catalog and inventory.
  • Full in-app browse, search, cart, and Shopify checkout — nothing bounces the customer to a browser.
  • Push notifications for drops, restocks, and sales — a direct channel that doesn't depend on email or ads.
  • Submitted, approved, and launched on both the App Store and Google Play — with ongoing maintenance after.
