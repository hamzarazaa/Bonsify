Why 79% of your Shopify traffic converts worse than the other 21%

Conversion · 8 min read

Most Shopify stores get the majority of their traffic on mobile and the majority of their revenue from desktop. That gap is the most expensive thing in the business, and almost nobody looks at it directly.

The number nobody frames correctly

When a store owner sees that mobile is the bulk of their sessions, the instinct is to feel good about it — mobile is where the customers are. But sessions aren't orders. The same customer who buys comfortably on a laptop hesitates on a phone, and the store rarely asks why.

The honest version of the stat isn't "we're mobile-first." It's "our best-converting surface is the one fewest people use."

The browser tab is the tax. Every return visit starts from a search bar instead of an icon.

Why the browser is the bottleneck

A mobile browser storefront asks more of a customer than a native app does, at exactly the moment they have the least patience. Pages load slower. There's no home screen presence, so the next visit begins with remembering the name and typing it in. There's no push channel, so the only way back is an ad or an email that competes with everything else in the inbox.

None of these are catastrophic on their own. Together, they're a slow leak — and the leak is widest on the surface most of your customers use.

What closes the gap

The fix isn't a faster theme or a new checkout button. It's changing where the store lives. When the store becomes an app on the home screen, the return visit is one tap, the load is instant, and there's a direct line to the customer that doesn't route through an algorithm.

That's the whole thesis behind going beyond the browser: stop optimizing the leaky surface, and move the customer to one that doesn't leak.

Where to start

You don't need a native app to test the idea. A home-screen install prompt — with a reason to install and a way to measure it — will tell you quickly whether your customers want to keep you on their phone. If they do, the native build pays for itself. If they don't, you've spent very little to find out.
