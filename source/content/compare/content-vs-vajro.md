Vajro, rebranded as Superfans in 2024, is a self-serve Shopify app builder that lets merchants assemble a native app from templates and manage it themselves. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, and builds and manages the app for the merchant instead of handing over a dashboard. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), and closing that gap is the reason most merchants start looking at either option.

## What Vajro (Superfans) does

Vajro gives merchants a builder interface and a set of templates, starting from $100 a month, with scaling fees as store volume grows. It works well for teams who want to configure their own app and iterate on it without waiting on outside help.

## What a done-for-you build does differently

A builder still requires someone on the merchant's side to configure screens, manage the push notification calendar, test each Shopify update against the app, resubmit to app stores, and troubleshoot when something breaks. That work does not disappear because the tool is self-serve. It just moves onto whoever on the team has time for it, which on most Shopify teams is nobody.

Bonsify runs that work as a build, not a subscription tool. The process has four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit alone answers the two questions merchants usually spend weeks debating on their own: whether an app makes sense for this store right now, and what it would actually cost. There is no fee to find out.

The build itself is native, not a webview wrapped around the mobile site. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer inside them per session (BuildFire). Push notifications, which are the main retention lever once the app is live, get open rates of 20 to 26 percent, compared to 15 to 20 percent for email. Bonsify sets up the push strategy as part of the build rather than leaving the merchant to figure out send cadence and segmentation from a template library.

Ownership is the other structural difference. With Bonsify, the client owns the app, the store listings, and all the data behind them. There are no success fees and no percentage of GMV, scoped or otherwise. The quote is fixed after the audit, so the cost does not move as the store grows.

Two live examples: BachaParty (bachaaparty.com), a kids' fashion store, and Highfy (highfy.pk), a beauty and skincare retailer, both run native iOS and Android apps built this way.

And an app is not the only lever. If the audit shows a store's real bottleneck is the theme, a broken checkout flow, or an outdated platform, Bonsify handles that too: store builds, Shopify migrations from WooCommerce or Magento, custom app development, and CRO work all sit under the same team.

## FAQ

**Is Bonsify more expensive than Vajro?**
Vajro's listed price is lower month to month, but it does not include the labor of configuring and maintaining the app. Bonsify's quote is fixed after the free audit and covers the full build, so the two are not priced on the same basis.

**Can I switch from Vajro to Bonsify?**
Yes. App rescue and migration from existing builders, including Vajro, is one of Bonsify's standard services.

**Does Bonsify only build apps?**
No. Apps are the flagship offering, but Bonsify also does Shopify store builds, platform migrations, custom app development, and store optimization.

**How long does the free audit take?**
A few days. It reviews the store's mobile conversion data and comes back with a scoped recommendation, not a generic pitch.

**Do I own the app if I work with Bonsify?**
Yes. The client owns the app, the app store listings, and all the underlying data.

**What if my store does not need a full native app?**
The audit will say so and point to what the store does need, whether that is a PWA, a theme rebuild, or a Shopify migration.
