StoreLab is a UK-based, budget-tier Shopify app builder with a basic feature set. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, built and managed by a team rather than configured by the merchant in a dashboard. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), and both approaches exist to close that gap.

## What StoreLab does

StoreLab's budget positioning makes it accessible for smaller merchants who want an app without a large commitment. Its basic feature set covers the core of what a template app needs to function.

## What a done-for-you build does differently

A basic feature set means a basic app: limited customization, limited push functionality, and limited room to adjust the app as a store's needs get more specific. Push notifications are one of the highest-leverage parts of running an app, with open rates of 20 to 26 percent against 15 to 20 percent for email, but that gap only closes when push is built around real triggers and segmentation, not a generic template setting. Bonsify builds the push strategy into the launch phase using the store's actual customer and order data.

Basic builders are also template-based rather than native by design in most cases, and template apps tend to run on webview, loading the mobile site inside a shell rather than running as standalone software. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), with users spending 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), a gap that comes from real software performance, not just app store presence.

Bonsify's process is four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit is free and tells a merchant in days whether an app is the right move at all, before any spend happens. Once built, ownership sits with the merchant: the app, the store listings, and all the data. Pricing is a fixed quote after the audit, with no success fees and no GMV percentage.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built this way, with ongoing support past launch.

For a smaller store weighing a budget app builder, the more useful question is often not app-or-not, it is what the audit actually finds. If a theme rebuild or store optimization would move the numbers more than an app right now, Bonsify handles that too, under the same team and the same free audit.

## Which one fits

StoreLab is a reasonable low-commitment option for a merchant who wants the lowest possible barrier to having an app. The free audit is the faster way to see what a fully built native app, or a different fix entirely, would look like for a specific store, scoped in days.

## FAQ

**Is StoreLab native or webview?**
It is a template-based builder, typically running on webview rather than native architecture.

**Why does a basic feature set matter for conversion?**
Limited customization and push functionality cap how much of the conversion and retention gap between mobile web and apps a basic build can actually close.

**Can I move from StoreLab to a native build?**
Yes. App rescue and migration from existing builders is a standard Bonsify service.

**Is Bonsify a good fit for smaller stores?**
The free audit scopes the quote to the store's actual size, so the fit is determined by the store's data, not a fixed enterprise price.

**Does Bonsify only build apps?**
No. Store design, optimization, and platform migrations are part of the broader service list.

**What does the free audit check?**
The store's real mobile conversion data, to determine what would actually move the numbers for that specific store.
