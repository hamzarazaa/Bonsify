Appbrew is a newer entrant in the Shopify app space that builds native apps through a self-serve platform. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, run as a managed build rather than a dashboard the merchant operates. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), and both approaches exist to close that gap.

## What Appbrew does

Appbrew builds native apps, which puts it ahead of the webview-based builders in the category on raw app performance. As a newer platform, it is iterating quickly on its feature set, which can mean faster access to new capabilities as it matures.

## What a done-for-you build does differently

Being newer also means a shorter track record. There are fewer years of merchants who have gone through a full build, launch, and multi-year maintenance cycle to point to, and app store rules, iOS and Android SDK requirements, and Shopify's own APIs all change often enough that a longer operating history matters when something breaks two years into running an app.

The self-serve model itself is the other variable. Even with a native app, someone still has to configure screens, manage the push calendar, and handle app store resubmissions when Shopify or Apple changes something. Bonsify runs that as a service instead of a tool: four phases, a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer, with Bonsify's team, not the merchant's, doing the ongoing work.

Push notifications are where a lot of the return shows up after launch. Open rates run 20 to 26 percent against 15 to 20 percent for email (industry benchmarks), but that only holds if the notification strategy is built around real store events rather than left as a generic feature to configure. Bonsify sets that strategy during the build, tied to the store's actual data.

Pricing is fixed after the free audit, with no success fees and no GMV percentage, so the number does not change as the store scales or as new features roll out.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built and maintained this way, with a support history that goes with them.

Bonsify's scope also extends past the app itself. App maintenance and support run as a retainer past launch, and if the audit surfaces a bigger issue, a slow theme, an outdated platform, that gets picked up under the same team rather than referred elsewhere.
