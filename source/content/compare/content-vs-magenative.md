MageNative is a template-heavy Shopify app builder starting from $49 a month, one of the lowest entry points in the category. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, and builds the app rather than handing over a template to configure. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), and that gap is what both approaches are aimed at.

## What MageNative does

MageNative's price makes it one of the most accessible ways to get an app live on Shopify, and for a merchant testing whether an app matters at all before committing more budget, that low bar to entry is a genuine reason to consider it.

## What a done-for-you build does differently

MageNative's apps run on webview, meaning the app loads the store's mobile site inside a native shell rather than running as native software. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and the gap comes largely from responsiveness and load speed, the exact areas where webview apps inherit whatever slowness already exists on the mobile site. Users spend 6.4 times longer per session in native apps than on mobile web (BuildFire), a difference that shows up mostly in how the app feels to use, not just what it looks like.

Template-heavy builders also cap what the app can actually do. Highly specific store needs, custom checkout flows, particular product configurators, tightly targeted push segmentation, tend to run into the edges of a template fast. Bonsify builds are not templated, they are scoped to the store during the free audit and built around what that specific store needs.

The process runs in four phases: audit, build, launch, and an optional support retainer, all handled by Bonsify's team rather than left to the merchant to configure and maintain inside a builder dashboard. Push notifications, which run 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, get built into the launch phase with real segmentation and triggers, not left as a template setting.

Bonsify's pricing is a fixed quote after the audit, with no success fees and no GMV percentage. It costs more upfront than MageNative's starting tier, and it reflects a build instead of a subscription.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built this way, not webview wraps.

If a store's real issue turns out to be the theme or checkout rather than the absence of an app, Bonsify's store design, optimization, and migration services cover that under the same team.

## FAQ

**Is MageNative a native app or webview?**
Webview. It loads the store's mobile site inside a native app shell.

**Why does webview vs native matter for conversion?**
Webview apps inherit the speed and responsiveness of the mobile site. Native apps run as standalone software and convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025).

**Can I move from MageNative to a native build?**
Yes. App rescue and migration from existing builders is a standard Bonsify service.

**Is Bonsify's price justified for a smaller store?**
The free audit scopes the quote to the store's actual size and needs, rather than a flat enterprise number.

**Does Bonsify only build apps?**
No. Store design, optimization, and platform migrations are also part of the service list.

**How fast is the free audit?**
A few days, based on the store's real mobile conversion data.
