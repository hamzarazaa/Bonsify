Shopney is a self-serve Shopify app builder with the lowest entry price in the category, starting from $99 a month. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, and builds the app as a managed project instead of a template a merchant configures. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), and that gap is what both approaches are trying to close.

## What Shopney does

Shopney is priced to be the easiest entry point into app ownership, and for a merchant who just wants something live with minimal spend, that low starting price is real. It comes with a template builder and basic push notification support.

## What a done-for-you build does differently

The tradeoff for Shopney's entry price shows up in push, which is the main retention channel once an app is live. Shopney's push capability is limited, and push open rates of 20 to 26 percent, against 15 to 20 percent for email, only materialize when the send calendar, segmentation, and triggers are built out properly. Bonsify sets up the full push strategy as part of the launch phase, not as a basic add-on to a builder.

The bigger gap is what happens after the app goes live. A template builder still needs someone to update screens when the store's catalog changes, test the app against every Shopify update, and manage app store resubmissions. That work does not go away because the monthly price is low, it just becomes an unpaid job for whoever on the team has time, which is usually nobody.

Bonsify's process runs in four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit costs nothing and tells a merchant in days whether a native app, a PWA, or something else is the right move, before any spend happens at all. Once built, apps are native, not templated webviews, and native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), with users spending 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire).

Pricing is fixed after the audit, with no success fees and no GMV percentage. It will not be the lowest number on the page, but it reflects the full build and the team behind it, not a subscription to a dashboard.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com), kids' fashion, and Highfy (highfy.pk), beauty and skincare, both run native apps built this way.

For stores where the audit points somewhere other than an app, Bonsify's scope covers Shopify store builds, migrations, custom development, and CRO under the same team.

## FAQ

**Why is Shopney cheaper than Bonsify?**
Shopney's price covers access to a builder dashboard the merchant configures. Bonsify's fixed quote covers the full build, launch, and setup, done by a team rather than by the merchant.

**Does Shopney support push notifications?**
Yes, but with limited functionality compared to a fully built-out push strategy.

**Can I move from Shopney to a native app later?**
Yes. App rescue and migration from existing builders is a standard Bonsify service.

**Is a low-cost builder ever the wrong move?**
The free audit is the fastest way to find out for a specific store, rather than guessing from price alone.

**What if my store just needs a better mobile site, not an app?**
The audit will say so. Bonsify also handles store builds, theme development, and CRO.

**How fast is the free audit?**
A few days, based on the store's actual mobile conversion data.
