Bonsify is a done-for-you Shopify services company that builds, launches and maintains
native mobile apps and PWAs for merchants. Tapcart is a no-code self-serve app builder
where merchants design and manage the app themselves. They solve the same end problem
(getting a store onto phones) through different models, and the right choice depends on
which constraint matters more to you: budget, or time.

This page compares the two honestly. We concede where Tapcart wins and explain where
done-for-you wins, so you can pick based on your situation and not on either company's
marketing page.

## Quick comparison

| | Bonsify (done-for-you) | Tapcart (self-serve builder) |
|---|---|---|
| Who builds the app | Bonsify's team | You, using a drag-and-drop editor |
| App architecture | Native iOS and Android, or PWA | Webview wrapper on iOS and Android |
| Time to launch | PWA: days. Native: several weeks | Days to a week with templates |
| Pricing model | Fixed scoped quote after free audit | From $200/month + performance fees at higher tiers |
| Success / GMV fees | None | Yes, on higher plans |
| Push notifications | Setup and strategy included | Self-serve; advanced segmentation is limited |

| App Store submission | Handled | Handled |
| Ongoing maintenance | Optional retainer | Included in SaaS subscription |
| Design | Custom to brand | Template-based blocks |
| You need to manage it? | No | Yes, someone on your team owns it |

## Where Tapcart wins

It is worth saying clearly because pretending otherwise would waste your time.

**Lower entry price.** Tapcart starts at $200 per month. A done-for-you build costs
more upfront. If budget is the binding constraint and you have internal capacity to
manage the app, Tapcart is the more accessible starting point.

**Faster launch.** A Tapcart app can be live in under a week using prebuilt blocks.
Native builds through Bonsify run several weeks. If speed to market matters more than
depth of build, Tapcart gets you there first.

**Self-serve control.** If you want to change a banner or swap a collection at midnight
without waiting on anyone, a builder gives you that. Done-for-you means you request
changes through the team, which is faster than custom dev but slower than doing it
yourself.

**Ecosystem of integrations.** Tapcart has built connections to Klaviyo, Yotpo,
LoyaltyLion, Recharge and others. The integration library is wide and growing.

## Where Bonsify wins

**No internal workload.** The most common feedback from merchants who leave builders is
that the app became someone's second job. Campaign setup, design updates, push
scheduling, listing maintenance. With done-for-you, the team that built the app also
runs it.

**Native builds, not webview wrappers.** Tapcart apps are built on a webview
architecture, which means the app is essentially your Shopify store rendered inside a
mobile shell. Pages load slower, animations feel less polished, and the experience is
bounded by what the mobile web can do. Bonsify builds natively, which means real device
performance.

**No surprise fee scaling.** Tapcart's higher tiers include performance-based fees tied
to in-app revenue. Merchants have reported price jumps from $200 to $400 to $1,000 as
they scale (Shopify App Store reviews, Head West Guide analysis). Bonsify quotes a fixed
scope; your revenue growth does not trigger a pricing event.

**Install strategy is part of the build.** Tapcart builds the app and stops. Getting
customers to actually install it is left to you. Bonsify includes the install layer
(reward on install, install-to-purchase attribution) because an app with no
installs earns nothing. This is the gap that separates "app exists" from "app works."

**The full Shopify stack.** Beyond mobile apps, Bonsify builds new Shopify stores,
migrates stores from other platforms, develops custom apps and themes, and handles
store optimization. If the audit reveals that mobile is not your bottleneck and a
store rebuild or migration is, the same team handles it without a referral chain.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Tapcart if:**
- Budget under $5,000 for mobile and a capable marketer with capacity to manage the app.
- Speed to launch is the priority and native performance is acceptable at "good enough."
- You want full self-serve control and are willing to learn the platform.

**Choose done-for-you (Bonsify) if:**
- Nobody on the team has bandwidth to own an app on top of everything else.
- Native performance matters (catalog-heavy stores, image-heavy brands, complex variants).
- You have scaled past the builder pricing tiers and the fees are eating into the margin the app was supposed to create.
- You want install strategy and attribution built in, not bolted on later.
- You need broader Shopify work (migration, store build, custom development) alongside or instead of an app.

## A note on switching

A meaningful number of Bonsify inquiries come from merchants who started on Tapcart or
another builder and outgrew it, either because the fees scaled, the webview performance
hit a ceiling, or the app became an unmanaged internal burden. Migration from a builder
to a native done-for-you build is a standard engagement for us, and the audit covers
what can be kept versus what needs rebuilding.

## FAQ

**Is Bonsify a competitor to Tapcart?**
Not directly. Tapcart is a software product you operate. Bonsify is a service that
operates for you. They solve the same end goal through different models. Some merchants
start on Tapcart and migrate to done-for-you later; the two are sequential as often as
they are alternatives.

**Is a Tapcart app native?**
No. Tapcart uses a webview architecture. The app renders your Shopify store inside a
mobile shell rather than building native screens. This affects load speed and the range
of interactions available.

**Does Tapcart charge success fees?**
On higher-tier plans, yes. Tapcart's pricing includes performance-based charges tied to
in-app GMV at the Ultimate and Enterprise levels (Tapcart pricing page; Head West Guide,
2025). Bonsify charges no percentage of sales on any engagement.
