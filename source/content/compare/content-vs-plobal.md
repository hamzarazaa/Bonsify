Plobal Apps is a self-serve Shopify app builder aimed at larger, enterprise-leaning stores, starting from $199 a month. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, and runs the build as a managed project rather than a dashboard the merchant configures alone. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), which is the gap both approaches are trying to close.

## What Plobal Apps does

Plobal targets bigger merchants with a webview-based builder and an enterprise feature set. It gives larger teams a way to stand up an app quickly using templates, and its focus on higher-volume stores means it comes with more configuration options out of the box than some lighter builders.

## What a done-for-you build does differently

Webview apps load the store's mobile site inside a native shell. That keeps build time short, but it also means app speed is capped by mobile site speed, and any mobile web issue, slow checkout, layout bugs, unoptimized images, follows the store straight into the app. A native build does not carry that ceiling. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), numbers that reflect the difference between a wrapped site and software built for the platform.

Plobal's enterprise focus also means enterprise-shaped commitments: onboarding calls, configuration time, and a team member who owns the app dashboard going forward. Bonsify runs that ownership question differently. The process is four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit tells a merchant in days, not weeks, whether an app is the right move and what it costs, before any commitment is made.

Push notifications are where the retention math actually plays out after launch. Open rates run 20 to 26 percent, well above email's 15 to 20 percent, but only if the send calendar and segmentation are set up properly. Bonsify builds that strategy in as part of the launch phase instead of leaving a merchant's team to learn it from a settings panel.

Pricing works differently too. Bonsify quotes a fixed price after the audit, with no success fees and no percentage of GMV. The number does not move as the store scales, which matters more for high-volume merchants, the exact profile Plobal targets, than for smaller ones.

Two stores running native apps built this way: BachaParty (bachaaparty.com), kids' fashion, and Highfy (highfy.pk), beauty and skincare retail.

Bonsify's scope also extends past the app. For larger, higher-volume merchants specifically, a Shopify Plus migration, headless or Hydrogen build, or B2B and wholesale setup is often part of the same conversation, and Bonsify handles all of it under one team.

## FAQ

**Is Plobal Apps webview or native?**
Webview. It loads the store's mobile site inside a native app shell rather than building native screens.

**Is Bonsify only for large or Shopify Plus stores?**
No. The free audit works the same way regardless of store size, and the fixed quote scales to the store's actual scope.

**Can I move from Plobal Apps to a native build?**
Yes. App rescue and migration from existing builders, including Plobal, is a standard Bonsify service.

**Does Bonsify handle Shopify Plus and headless builds?**
Yes. Shopify Plus migration, headless and Hydrogen development, and B2B and wholesale setup are part of the broader service list alongside apps.

**How is Bonsify priced compared to Plobal's enterprise tiers?**
Bonsify quotes a fixed price after the free audit with no success fees or GMV percentage, so cost does not increase as the store scales the way tiered enterprise pricing can.

**What does the free audit actually check?**
The store's real mobile conversion and traffic data, to determine whether an app, a PWA, or something else entirely is the right next step.
