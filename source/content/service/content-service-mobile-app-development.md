## Why native over a template builder

Self-serve builders like Tapcart, Vajro, or Shopney give a merchant a dashboard to configure, which means someone on the team has to build screens, manage push, and handle app store resubmissions as an ongoing job. A done-for-you native build removes that ongoing configuration work, and because it isn't templated, it isn't capped by what a template supports. Users spend 6.4 times longer per session in native apps than on mobile web (BuildFire), and push notifications run 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, both of which depend on the app being built around the store's real behavior rather than a generic setting.
