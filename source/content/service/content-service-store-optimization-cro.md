## Why CRO often outperforms a full rebuild

Not every store needs a full redesign or a new app to see a real conversion lift. A slow product page, a checkout with too many steps, or a mobile layout that doesn't fit the way customers actually browse can each cost meaningful conversion on their own, and fixing them is faster and cheaper than a full rebuild. Bonsify's free audit identifies which of these apply to a specific store before recommending anything larger.

CRO work is also where a lot of the value from a later mobile app gets protected. An app built on top of an unoptimized store inherits the same friction points, so addressing checkout speed and page performance first, or alongside an app build, tends to compound the return on both investments.
