## Why internationalization is more than a currency switcher

A basic currency converter on a store isn't the same as real internationalization. Customers expect prices that look native to their market, not a converted number with the wrong formatting. They expect checkout, shipping options, and confirmation emails in their language, not just the product pages. And they expect the payment methods common in their region, not a default set built around one market. Getting any of these wrong creates the kind of friction that shows up as abandoned checkouts specifically from international traffic.

Bonsify's internationalization work covers the full customer journey in each target market, not just a language toggle on the homepage, so international traffic converts closer to the rate of the store's home market rather than well below it.

No. Proper internationalization includes localized checkout, translated transactional content, and region-specific payment methods, not just a converted price display.
