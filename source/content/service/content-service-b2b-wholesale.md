## Why B2B needs a different setup than standard D2C

Business buyers order differently than consumers: larger quantities, negotiated or tiered pricing, payment terms instead of immediate checkout, and often a need to reorder the same items quickly without rebuilding a cart from scratch each time. A store configured only for individual consumer checkout makes wholesale buyers work around functionality that isn't built for them, which shows up as friction, or as buyers going around the online store entirely and ordering by phone or email instead.

Bonsify configures B2B functionality around how the store's actual wholesale buyers order, whether that's Shopify's native B2B features or custom development for requirements those features don't cover, like specific payment term structures or approval workflows.
