## Why submission and ASO are both part of the same problem

Getting an app approved and getting it found are two different challenges that both determine whether the app actually drives sales. Apple's and Google's review processes have specific, sometimes shifting requirements around metadata, permissions, and functionality, and a rejected or delayed submission pushes back the entire launch. Once approved, an app with no ASO work behind it can sit invisible in search results even for customers actively looking for the brand's own app by name.

Bonsify handles both as part of the launch phase, not as separate line items. Submission includes preparing everything App Store and Google Play require and managing the review cycle. ASO includes researching what customers actually search for, optimizing the app's title, description, and keywords accordingly, and testing icon and screenshot variations for conversion once the listing is live.
