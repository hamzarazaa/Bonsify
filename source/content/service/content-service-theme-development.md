## Why theme quality affects more than looks

A theme isn't just visual design, it's the code that determines how fast pages load, how smoothly checkout flows, and how well the store performs on mobile specifically. A stock theme with cosmetic changes carries whatever performance limitations the template has, and those limitations show up directly in conversion rate, particularly on mobile where load speed and simplicity matter more than on desktop.

Bonsify builds themes from the brand and catalog outward rather than starting from a template and customizing around its constraints. That includes how products are categorized and filtered, how the cart and checkout flow work, and specific mobile performance work, since that's where most traffic and the widest conversion gap both live.
