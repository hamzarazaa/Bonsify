## When headless makes sense

Headless and Hydrogen development is not the right starting point for most Shopify stores. It's a bigger technical investment than theme development, and it makes the most sense for stores with specific performance requirements a standard theme can't meet, or a front-end experience that genuinely requires custom architecture rather than theme customization. Bonsify's free audit assesses this honestly rather than defaulting to headless when a well-built custom theme would solve the same problem faster and cheaper.

For stores that do need it, headless architecture offers a real performance ceiling above standard themes, since the front-end isn't constrained by Shopify's theme rendering layer, while still keeping Shopify's checkout and backend, which most merchants don't want to rebuild.

## FAQ

**Do I need headless, or would a custom theme work for my store?**
For most stores, a well-built custom theme solves the same problems faster and cheaper. Headless makes sense for specific performance or front-end requirements a standard theme can't meet, which the scoping conversation assesses honestly.

**Does headless mean losing Shopify's checkout?**
No, headless and Hydrogen development typically keeps Shopify's backend for checkout, inventory, and customer data while customizing the front-end.

**How long does a headless project take?**
Timeline depends on the project's scope and is defined during the scoping conversation.

**Is headless development more expensive than a custom theme?**
Generally yes, given the added technical complexity, which is why it's scoped carefully rather than recommended by default.

**Does Bonsify maintain headless builds after launch?**
Yes, ongoing development and maintenance is available as the build evolves.

**Is headless a common fit for Shopify Plus stores?**
It comes up more often for larger merchants with higher performance requirements, though the fit depends on the store's specific needs rather than store size alone.
