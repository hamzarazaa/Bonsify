## Why native iOS matters

Webview-based builders load the store's mobile site inside an iOS app shell, which means the app's performance is capped by mobile site speed rather than running as real iOS software. A native build works directly with iOS frameworks, so it loads faster and feels closer to the rest of a customer's phone. Users spend 6.4 times longer per session in native apps than on mobile web (BuildFire), and push notifications, worth 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work more reliably when they're built on native iOS push infrastructure rather than a wrapped web notification.

Apple's App Store review process is also a real part of shipping an iOS app, with specific requirements around metadata, screenshots, and functionality. Bonsify manages that submission as part of the build, rather than leaving a merchant to navigate App Store guidelines alone for the first time.
