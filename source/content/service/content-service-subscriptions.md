## Why subscription setup is more than installing an app

Installing a subscription app is only the first step. The pricing structure, the cadence options offered, the churn recovery flow when a payment fails, and how customers manage their own subscriptions all affect whether the program actually retains customers or quietly leaks them. A subscription program with confusing management options or no recovery flow for failed payments loses revenue that a properly configured setup would keep.

Bonsify configures subscription setup around the store's specific product type: how often a supplement bottle or a beauty product typically needs reordering, what discount structure incentivizes staying subscribed without eroding margin, and how renewal reminders integrate with the store's broader push and email strategy.
