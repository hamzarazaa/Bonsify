## Why store setup is often the real first step

Some merchants come to Bonsify assuming they need a mobile app, and the free audit finds instead that the store itself, the theme, the checkout flow, the page speed, is the actual bottleneck. An app built on top of a slow or poorly converting store inherits those same problems. Getting the store right first, whether that's a new build or a redesign of an existing one, is often what makes every later investment, including a future app, actually pay off.

Bonsify's store builds are custom, not template installations with a new logo. That means design decisions get made around the specific brand and product catalog: how products are categorized, how checkout is structured, and how the store performs on mobile specifically, where the bulk of traffic usually lands.
