## Why native Android matters

Webview-based builders load the store's mobile site inside an Android app shell, which caps performance at mobile site speed. That gap matters more on Android than on iOS, since Android runs across a much wider range of devices and screen sizes, and a webview wrapper handles that variability worse than software built natively for the platform. Users spend 6.4 times longer per session in native apps than on mobile web (BuildFire), and push notifications, worth 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, run more reliably on native Android push infrastructure than through a wrapped web notification layer.

Google Play's review process is faster than Apple's in most cases, but it still requires proper metadata, permissions handling, and testing across device types. Bonsify manages that submission as part of the build.
