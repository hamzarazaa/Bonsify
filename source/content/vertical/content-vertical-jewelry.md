## Why jewelry has the widest mobile gap in ecommerce

Jewelry purchases involve real deliberation: comparing pieces, checking sizing, and often researching a brand's return policy before committing to a higher-than-average price point. That deliberation is exactly what mobile browsers handle worst, small screens, easy tab-switching, and a checkout flow that's simple to abandon mid-decision. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), and for a category built on trust and gifting, that gap has an outsized cost given the $130 average order value.

## What a native app changes for jewelry brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), time that matters directly for a category where customers browse, save, and return before buying. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work well for jewelry around gifting occasions, wishlist reminders, and new collection launches, all of which fit a considered-purchase category better than one-off promotional blasts.
