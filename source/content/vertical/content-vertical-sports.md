## Why sports and fitness has room to close the mobile gap

Fitness customers are among the most mobile-native shoppers in ecommerce, already used to apps for workouts, tracking, and community, which makes a slow or generic mobile browsing experience feel more out of place here than in most categories. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), a gap that costs more in a category where repeat purchases, replacement gear, supplements, apparel refreshes, drive a large share of revenue.

## What a native app changes for sports and fitness brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), both relevant for a customer base that already expects an app-native experience from the rest of their fitness routine. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work well for sports and fitness around new gear drops, restocks, and seasonal training cycles.
