## Why beauty loses more on mobile than most categories

Beauty shopping on mobile involves a lot of scanning: shade matching, ingredient checking, and comparing across product lines, all of which get harder on a small screen inside a browser tab that's easy to abandon mid-scroll. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), and beauty's repeat-purchase nature makes that abandonment gap compound every reorder cycle, not just the first purchase.

## What a native app changes for a beauty brand

Replenishment is where beauty apps earn their keep. Push notifications timed to a customer's typical reorder window, a skincare routine running low, a shade about to sell out, run at 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email. That is a direct line to the customers most likely to buy again, without competing for space in an inbox.

Native apps also convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web overall (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), time that matters in a category built on browsing, comparing, and building a routine rather than one-off purchases.
