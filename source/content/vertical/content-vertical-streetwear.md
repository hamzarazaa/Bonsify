## Why drops are won or lost on notification speed

A drop's entire sales window can close in minutes, sometimes seconds, once high-demand items go live. Email, with 15 to 20 percent open rates and no guarantee of being read in time, is too slow for that window. A customer browsing a mobile site has to notice the drop happened, load the page, and check out, all while inventory disappears. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), and for a drop-based brand, that mobile web abandonment often means the item sold out before the customer even finished loading the page.

## What a native app changes for streetwear and drop brands

Push notifications run at 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, and for a drop-based brand, the difference is not just open rate, it's speed. A push notification lands on a locked screen instantly; an email sits in an inbox behind dozens of others. Native apps also convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), with a checkout flow fast enough to matter when inventory is measured in minutes, not days.
