## Why pet supplies rewards a fast reorder path

Pet owners run out of food, litter, and treats on a predictable cycle, and the purchase decision at that point is rarely comparative shopping, it is getting the same product again as quickly as possible. A slow mobile checkout costs a sale that would otherwise happen automatically. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), a gap that shows up on every reorder cycle in a category built on repeat purchases.

## What a native app changes for pet supply brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), both relevant for a category where customers browse new products, treats, toys, seasonal items, between routine reorders. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work well for pet supplies: a bag of food due to run low, a subscription box arriving, or a new product line launching are all natural, schedulable triggers.
