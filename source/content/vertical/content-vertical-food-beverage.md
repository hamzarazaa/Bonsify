## Why food and beverage loses sales on mobile

Food and beverage purchases tend to be fast decisions, often made when a customer is already thinking about a specific product, a coffee subscription running low, a snack they want again. A slow-loading mobile site or a multi-step checkout breaks that impulse before it converts. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), and in a category driven by quick, habitual purchases, that gap shows up every single reorder cycle.

## What a native app changes for food and beverage brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), a meaningful lift for a category where AOV is comparatively low and volume matters more than any single order size. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work particularly well for food and beverage: reorder reminders timed to typical consumption cycles, limited seasonal items, and flash restocks all fit the format naturally.
