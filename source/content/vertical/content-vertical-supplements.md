## Why supplements depend on reorder speed

Supplements are consumed on a schedule, which means the purchase decision is rarely a discovery moment, it is a "running low" moment. If reordering on mobile takes more than a couple of taps, that habitual purchase is exactly the kind of transaction likely to get abandoned mid-checkout. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), a gap that compounds every reorder cycle for a subscription-heavy category.

## What a native app changes for supplement brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), both relevant for a category where customers often research ingredients and dosing before buying, then return repeatedly afterward. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, are a natural fit for supplement reorder timing: a 30-day supply running low is a predictable, schedulable trigger, not a guess.
