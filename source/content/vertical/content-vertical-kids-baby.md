## Why kids and baby rewards a fast mobile experience

Parents shopping for kids and baby products are frequently repeat buyers on a schedule: sizes change, supplies run out, and gift-giving occasions repeat every year. That makes the category unusually dependent on how easy it is to come back and buy again quickly, on a phone, often one-handed. Mobile web converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent across ecommerce generally (Dynamic Yield, 2025), well under the 7.5 percent top-decile figure kids and baby stores can reach with the right mobile setup.

## What a native app changes for a kids and baby brand

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), both of which matter for a category where repeat visits and quick reorders drive a large share of revenue. Push notifications, run at 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work well for restock alerts, size-up reminders, and seasonal drops, all natural fits for how parents actually shop this category.
