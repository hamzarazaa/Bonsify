## Why electronics loses more on mobile than most categories

Electronics purchases involve comparison shopping: specs across models, compatibility checks, and reviews, all research that mobile browsers make slower and easier to abandon than desktop. With an average order value of $180, that abandonment is expensive. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), and for a category built on research before purchase, losing that customer mid-comparison on a slow mobile site is one of the more costly gaps in ecommerce.

## What a native app changes for electronics brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), directly relevant for a category where customers return multiple times to compare before buying. A faster, native comparison and product detail experience keeps that research inside the app instead of losing the customer to a competitor's tab. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, work well for electronics around price drops, restock alerts on high-demand items, and new model launches.
