## Why footwear loses sales on mobile

Footwear purchases carry sizing uncertainty that most other product categories don't: half sizes, fit differences between models, and return policy questions all get checked before a customer commits. That research is slower and easier to abandon on a mobile browser than in a purpose-built app. Cart abandonment runs around 85 percent on mobile web versus roughly 20 percent inside apps (Barilliance; Venn Apps), a gap that compounds for footwear brands running frequent releases or size-limited drops.

## What a native app changes for footwear brands

Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire), both relevant for a category where customers browse, compare sizing, and often return before completing a purchase. Push notifications, running 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, are especially effective for footwear around restocks on specific sizes, new colorway releases, and limited drops, moments where speed of notification directly affects whether a customer gets the size they want.
