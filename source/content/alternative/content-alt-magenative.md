## Why merchants look for a MageNative alternative

MageNative's starting price of $49 a month makes it one of the cheapest ways to get an app live, useful for merchants testing the waters before committing more budget. Merchants tend to look elsewhere once the webview architecture and template limits start capping what the app can actually do, particularly around push segmentation and store-specific features.

## What switching to Bonsify looks like

Bonsify's process runs in four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit is free and scopes what a native build would look like in days.

Because MageNative runs on webview, the app inherits whatever speed and responsiveness issues exist on the mobile site. A native build does not carry that ceiling. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), with users spending 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire). Push, worth 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, is built into the launch phase with real segmentation rather than a template setting.

Switching from MageNative is handled through Bonsify's app rescue and migration service.

Pricing is a fixed quote after the audit, with no success fees or GMV percentage. It costs more than MageNative's entry tier, and it reflects a full native build rather than a template subscription.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built this way, not webview wraps.

Bonsify's scope also covers what a store might need instead of, or before, an app: theme rebuilds, optimization, and platform migrations, under the same team.

## FAQ

**Can I keep my app data when switching from MageNative?**
App rescue and migration is a standard Bonsify service that carries a store's existing setup into the new build.

**Is MageNative's low price ever the right call?**
For a merchant testing whether an app matters at all before committing more budget, it is a real option.

**Why does webview matter for app performance?**
Webview apps inherit the speed of the mobile site underneath them. Native apps run as standalone software and convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025).

**Does Bonsify only replace app builders?**
