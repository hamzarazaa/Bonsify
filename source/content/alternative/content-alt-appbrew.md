## Why merchants look for an Appbrew alternative

Appbrew builds native apps through a self-serve platform, which puts it ahead of webview-based builders on raw performance, and as a newer entrant it moves quickly on new features. Merchants tend to look elsewhere once the self-serve dashboard becomes an ongoing job, or once a shorter operating history starts to matter, particularly two or three years into running an app when app store rules and Shopify's APIs have shifted several times.

## What switching to Bonsify looks like

Bonsify's process runs in four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer, with Bonsify's own team handling ongoing work rather than the merchant. The audit is free and scopes the build in days.

Push notifications, worth 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, get built around the store's real data during the launch phase. Once live, the app is backed by an optional support retainer, so updates, resubmissions, and troubleshooting stay with Bonsify rather than falling back on the merchant's team.

Switching from Appbrew is handled through Bonsify's app rescue and migration service, moving the store's existing app history into the new build.

Pricing is a fixed quote after the audit, with no success fees or GMV percentage. Ownership of the app, listings, and data stays with the merchant.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built and maintained this way, with a multi-year support history behind them.

Bonsify's scope also runs past the app itself: if the audit surfaces a bigger issue, like an outdated theme or platform, that gets picked up under the same team.

**Can I keep my app data when switching from Appbrew?**
App rescue and migration is a standard Bonsify service that carries a store's existing setup into the new build.

**Is Appbrew's native architecture a real advantage over other builders?**
Yes, native apps generally outperform webview apps. The question for merchants switching is usually about ongoing maintenance and track record rather than app architecture itself.

**Who handles updates after switching to Bonsify?**
Bonsify, through an optional support retainer, rather than the merchant's own team.

**How long does switching from Appbrew take?**
Timeline is scoped during the free audit based on the store's current setup.

**Does Bonsify only replace app builders?**
No. Store builds, migrations, and custom development are also part of the service list.

**What if my store's issue is not really about the app builder?**
The audit identifies that and points to the actual fix, whether that is a theme rebuild or something else.
