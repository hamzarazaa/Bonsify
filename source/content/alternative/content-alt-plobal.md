A Plobal Apps alternative, in practical terms, means moving from a self-serve enterprise builder dashboard to a service that builds and manages the app instead. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, and is one such alternative. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), the gap merchants are usually trying to close either way.

## Why merchants look for a Plobal Apps alternative

Plobal targets larger, higher-volume merchants with a webview-based builder starting from $199 a month, and its enterprise feature set is a real strength for teams that want to configure a lot of options themselves. Merchants tend to look elsewhere when the ongoing dashboard ownership, onboarding time, and webview performance ceiling start costing more attention than the team has to give.

## What switching to Bonsify looks like

Bonsify runs the process in four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit is free and answers, in days, whether an app is worth building and what it would cost, before anything is committed.

Because the build is native rather than a webview wrapper, it is not capped by mobile site speed the way webview apps are. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire). Push notifications, worth 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, get set up during the launch phase around the store's real order and customer data.

Switching from Plobal is handled as a standard migration, not a special case. Bonsify's app rescue and migration service moves the store's existing setup into the new build.

Pricing is a fixed quote after the audit, with no success fees and no GMV percentage, which tends to matter more for the higher-volume stores Plobal targets than for smaller ones, since the number does not climb with scale.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built this way. For higher-volume merchants specifically, Shopify Plus migration, headless or Hydrogen builds, and B2B or wholesale setup often come up in the same conversation, and Bonsify handles all of it.
