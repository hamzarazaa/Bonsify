A Shopify app builder alternative is any option outside the standard self-serve dashboard model, ranging from other builders with different pricing and features to done-for-you services that build and manage the app for the merchant. Bonsify is a Shopify services company by FetchSky that builds native iOS and Android apps, PWAs, new Shopify stores, platform migrations, custom apps, and more, and represents the done-for-you category. Mobile web on Shopify converts at roughly 2.1 to 2.9 percent, against 3.5 to 3.9 percent on desktop (Dynamic Yield, 2025), which is the gap every option on this list is trying to close.

## The self-serve builders

**Tapcart** is the best-known name in the category, starting from $200 a month, with success fees kicking in at higher tiers. It runs on webview and works for merchants comfortable managing an app dashboard themselves.

**Vajro**, rebranded as Superfans in 2024, starts from $100 a month with scaling fees as store volume grows, and offers a similar self-serve template model.

**Plobal Apps** starts from $199 a month and targets larger, higher-volume merchants with a more enterprise-leaning feature set, also running on webview.

**Shopney** has the lowest entry price in the category at $99 a month, with a basic template builder and more limited push notification functionality.

**MobiLoud** is not Shopify-specific. It wraps any website in a native shell, which suits multi-platform businesses more than Shopify-only stores.

**Appbrew** is a newer entrant that builds native, rather than webview, apps through a self-serve platform, with a shorter track record than more established players.

**MageNative** starts from $49 a month, the cheapest tier on this list, and is template-heavy, typically running on webview.

**StoreLab** is a UK-based, budget-tier builder with a basic feature set.

Every builder on this list follows the same core model: the merchant configures the app, manages the push calendar, and handles ongoing updates. That works well for teams with the time and technical comfort to treat it as an internal responsibility.

## The done-for-you alternative

Bonsify sits outside the builder model entirely. Instead of a dashboard, the process runs in four phases: a free audit, the build, launch, and an optional support retainer. The audit costs nothing and answers, in days, whether an app is the right move and what it would cost, before any commitment happens.

The build itself is native, not templated, so it is not capped by webview performance the way most builders on this list are. Native apps convert about 130 percent higher than mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025), and users spend 6.4 times longer per session inside them (BuildFire). Push notifications, worth 20 to 26 percent open rates against 15 to 20 percent for email, are built into the launch phase around the store's real order and customer data, not left as a dashboard setting.

Pricing is a fixed quote after the audit, with no success fees and no GMV percentage, so the number does not move as the store scales. Ownership of the app, the store listings, and the data stays with the merchant throughout.

BachaParty (bachaaparty.com) and Highfy (highfy.pk) both run native apps built this way. And switching from any of the builders above is a standard service through Bonsify's app rescue and migration process, which carries the store's existing setup into the new build.

Bonsify's scope also runs past mobile apps specifically. Shopify store builds, platform migrations, custom app development, and CRO are part of the same team's work, which matters if the real fix for a given store turns out to be something other than an app.
