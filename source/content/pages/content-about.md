## Section 1: Why we exist

Most Shopify merchants know two things at once: most of their traffic is on phones, and most of their revenue is not. Roughly 75% of ecommerce sessions are mobile (Statista, 2025), yet the mobile web converts at about half the desktop rate (Dynamic Yield, 2025).

The fix has existed for years. Apps convert around 130% higher than the mobile web (Venn Apps, 2025). What has not existed is a sane way for a mid-sized store to get one. The DIY builders hand you a template and a second job. Custom development starts at six figures. Bonsify was built for the merchants in between: agency-grade native apps, delivered as a service, without the enterprise invoice or the internal workload.

## Section 2: Where we come from

Bonsify is a product of FetchSky, a software company that has spent ten years building and operating products used by real businesses. That matters for one practical reason: we run apps after launch, not just before it. OS updates, Shopify API changes, push infrastructure, app store policies. A decade of operating software is the difference between a launch partner and a long-term one.

We also build our own products on the same stack we sell. Dockify, our Shopify App Store app, adds a home screen install prompt with a reward and install-to-purchase attribution. We built it because we kept watching well-made apps fail at the install step. When we recommend an install strategy to a client, it is one we run ourselves.

## Section 3: How we work (short version, links to How We Work page)

Audit, build, launch, support. One team, one accountable party, from the first review of your store to the update that ships two years later. No handoffs, no half-finished apps. The full engagement model is on the How We Work page.

## FAQ (About page)

**Who is behind Bonsify?**
Bonsify is built and operated by FetchSky, a software product company founded a decade ago, with teams in the United States and Pakistan.

**Is Bonsify an app builder like Tapcart?**
No. Builders are self-serve software you configure yourself. Bonsify is done-for-you: we build, launch and maintain the app for you.

**Where is Bonsify located?**
Santa Ana, California (US) and Karachi (Pakistan). Client work is remote-first regardless of location.

**Do you only work with Shopify stores?**
Yes. Specializing in one platform is why the builds are fast and the integrations are deep.
