# What to record

Same room, same mic, same framing, same lighting for both clips. 1080p landscape, 30 fps,
head and shoulders, plain background. Leave one second of stillness before you start and after you finish.
The intro gets a "Made for <brand>" tag added automatically, so do not say the prospect's name.

## Intro (15 to 20 seconds, about 50 words)

> Hi, I'm [Name] from Bonsify. We turn Shopify stores into mobile apps, and I've put together a quick walkthrough of what yours could look like. Everything you're about to see is running on your real products. It takes about two minutes, so let's get into it.

## Outro (5 to 10 seconds, about 27 words)

> That's your app. Scan the code or tap the link to open it on your own phone. I'm [Name], and I'd love to hear what you'd change.

## Voice sample (about 2 minutes, read once, separate file)

This trains the clone. Read it in the same tone you will use in the clips: warm, steady, not rushed.
One take, no music, no echo, no long gaps. Save as `voice-sample.mp3` or `.wav`.

> This is your store as a mobile app. It is not a mockup. It is running on your real catalogue, and you can try it on your own phone at the end of this video. The home screen opens on your banners and your best sellers. Shoppers jump straight to the category they want, and today's deals sit right under the fold. Open a product and you get a full gallery, the price, the saving, and delivery dates. Pick an option, tap add to cart, and it is in the bag. Right below, frequently bought together puts the matching items one tap away. That is how a single order turns into a bigger one. The cart shows loyalty points, instalments and your vouchers, and checkout stays short. Your existing payment setup keeps working as it does today. Reviews sit on every product, and a chat button puts your team one tap from the shopper. Both are on screen when the doubt shows up. An app lives on the home screen. You can send an order update, a restock alert or a sale as a push notification, and shoppers come back without paying for the click again. We build it on top of your existing Shopify store. Products, orders and customers stay in Shopify, so there is nothing to migrate and nothing to rebuild. We keep it running afterwards. Scan the code on screen, or use the link below, to open this exact app on your phone. Tap around. Then tell us what you would change.

Get a short written consent from the person whose voice it is. ElevenLabs asks you to confirm you have the right to clone it.
